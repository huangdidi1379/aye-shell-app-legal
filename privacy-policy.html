面具 App 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年5月17日

面具（以下简称"本应用"）尊重并保护所有用户的个人隐私。本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理用户信息。

1. 信息收集

本应用不收集任何个人身份信息。具体而言：

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码等身份信息
  • 不收集设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）
  • 不收集位置信息
  • 不收集通讯录或照片
  • 不使用 Cookie 或任何第三方追踪技术

2. 数据存储

所有用户数据（面具记录、心理分析结果等）均存储在用户本地设备上，使用 Apple 原生数据存储框架（SwiftData/CoreData）。数据不会上传至任何服务器。

3. 购买信息

本应用通过 Apple App Store 提供应用内购买。购买交易由 Apple 直接处理，本应用不存储任何支付信息（如信用卡号）。购买凭证使用 iOS Keychain 安全存储，而非普通存储。

4. 数据删除

用户可以随时通过以下方式删除所有数据：

  • 在应用内删除单条面具记录
  • 卸载应用将自动删除所有本地数据

卸载后，所有数据将被永久删除且无法恢复。

5. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方分析服务、广告网络或社交平台 SDK。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. GDPR 合规（欧盟用户）

根据《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR），欧盟用户享有以下权利：

  • 访问权：您有权了解本应用存储了哪些数据（均为您本地设备上的数据）
  • 删除权：您可以随时删除应用中的所有数据
  • 数据可携带权：由于数据仅存储在本地设备，您可以通过 iOS 系统备份功能导出数据
  • 拒绝权：本应用不进行任何自动化决策或用户画像分析（除应用内提供的心理分析功能外）

如需行使上述权利，只需在应用内删除相关数据或卸载应用即可。

本应用不进行个人数据跨境传输，因为数据从未离开您的设备。

8. CCPA 合规（加州用户）

根据《加州消费者隐私法》（CCPA），加州居民享有以下权利：

  • 知道收集了哪些个人信息的权利（本应用不收集个人信息）
  • 删除个人信息的权利
  • 拒绝出售个人信息的权利（本应用不出售任何信息）

本应用不出售、交易或以其他方式向外部各方传输用户信息。

9. 隐私政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在应用内或本页面发布。继续使用本应用即表示您同意修订后的政策。

Shell App Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Shell (hereinafter referred to as "this App") respects and protects the personal privacy of all users. This Privacy Policy explains how this App handles user information.

1. Information Collection

This App does not collect any personal identification information. Specifically:

  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other identity information
  • Does not collect device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV)
  • Does not collect location information
  • Does not collect contacts or photos
  • Does not use cookies or any third-party tracking technologies

2. Data Storage

All user data (mask records, psychological analysis results, etc.) is stored locally on the user's device using Apple's native data storage frameworks (SwiftData/CoreData). Data is never uploaded to any server.

3. Purchase Information

This App offers in-app purchases through the Apple App Store. Purchase transactions are processed directly by Apple. This App does not store any payment information (such as credit card numbers). Purchase receipts are securely stored using iOS Keychain, not regular storage.

4. Data Deletion

Users can delete all data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual mask records within the App
  • Uninstalling the App, which automatically deletes all local data

After uninstallation, all data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

5. Third-Party Services

This App does not integrate any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or social platform SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), EU users have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to know what data this App stores (all data is on your local device only)
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all data in the App at any time
  • Right to Data Portability: Since data is stored only on your local device, you can export data through iOS system backup functionality
  • Right to Object: This App does not engage in any automated decision-making or user profiling (other than the psychological analysis feature provided within the App)

To exercise these rights, simply delete the relevant data within the App or uninstall the App.

This App does not transfer personal data across borders, as data never leaves your device.

8. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the following rights:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected (this App does not collect personal information)
  • The right to delete personal information
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (this App does not sell any information)

This App does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer user information to external parties.

9. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted within the App or on this page. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
